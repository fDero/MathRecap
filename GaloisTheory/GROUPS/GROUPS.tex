\section{Galois-related group theory}
In this section, we will explore the concepts of advanced group theory that are related to 
Galois theory. In particular, we will focus on the symmetric group, the alternating group, and the
concept of normal-subgroup and quotient-groups. We will define what does it mean for a group to be 
Galos-solvable and we will prove that the so called symmetric group $S_n$ is not solvable for $n \geq 5$. This 
will later be linked to the fact that there are polynomial equations of degree $n \geq 5$ 
that are not solvable in radicals.
