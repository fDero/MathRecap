\section{Fields and automorphisms}
In this section we will explain the concept of field extensions of a given field, we 
will define different types of field extensions (e.g. normal-extension, galois-extension,
radical-extension) and we will see how they are related to a particular group called 
Galois group. We will herby lay the foundation for the next chapter that will treat the 
connection between field extensions and solvibility of polynomial equations.
