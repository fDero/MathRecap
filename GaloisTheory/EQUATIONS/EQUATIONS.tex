\section{Solvability of polynomial equations}
In this chapter we will finally prove the main result of this notes, which is 
the fact that there is no general method to solve polynomial equations of degree
five or higher. We will do this by showing that the Galois group of a
polynomial of degree five or higher is not "Galois-solvable", while also explaining 
how this is connected to the solvability of the general polynomial equation. \\
